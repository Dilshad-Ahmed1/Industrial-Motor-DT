## Verification limits and manuscript policy

- Crossref/OpenAlex metadata and abstracts were used for the two MDPI motor-diagnosis papers. The publisher pages were inaccessible to the retrieval environment (HTTP 403), so their methods/results sections were not independently reviewed. The manuscript makes no quantitative comparative claim based on them.
- The Staton et al., Benbouzid, Liu and Bazzi, and Kritzinger records were checked for bibliographic metadata; full texts were not reviewed. The manuscript uses only high-level statements supported by metadata/abstract-level information.
- The MSL, FMI, and AAS sources are standards/library documentation, not experimental validation evidence.
- No claim of novelty, first use, state-of-the-art superiority, physical validation, industrial deployment, or hardware real-time capability is based on this matrix.
- Human authors should inspect the full texts, confirm the relevance and reference details, and review the final wording before submission.
